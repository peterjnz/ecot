_How a Process Control Engineer turned CISO Operationalized OT Cyber Policy_

_A Manitoba Hydro Case Study on Pragmatic Cyber Policy in Critical Infrastructure_

# Date

Mar 20, 2026 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM Coordinated Universal Time

# Summary

Dawn Nedohin-Macek outlined Manitoba Hydro’s shift from fragmented, IT-centric policies to a unified cybersecurity program spanning IT and OT. The initiative focused on making policies practical, enforceable, and relevant to real operations by removing aspirational language, engaging cross-functional stakeholders, and clearly separating policy from procedures. This resulted in a streamlined policy set, improved governance and accountability, and mechanisms for ongoing feedback and continuous improvement.

The discussion focused on how to operationalize policy in practice, including enforcing compliance, handling legacy constraints, and driving behavioral change. Dawn highlighted the importance of publishing early, using non-compliance as a leading indicator, and leveraging tiered responses to engage staff and leadership. Members explored challenges around IT/OT ownership, resourcing, and cultural alignment, with strong emphasis on collaboration, pragmatic implementation, and using policy as a lever to enable rather than hinder operations.
